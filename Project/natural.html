Natural Gas and Energy Efficiency

Homes and businesses that use natural gas for heating, cooking, and hot water may be more energy efficient than those relying solely on electricity—especially when you consider how energy is created and delivered.

Unlike electricity, which can lose significant energy during generation and transmission, natural gas delivers energy directly to your appliances with minimal loss. That efficiency means natural gas customers in Colorado save an average of 52% compared to those who use electricity.*

Since 1990, methane emissions from the gas utility industry have declined by 69%.** As Colorado continues to focus on energy efficiency and reducing emissions, natural gas can be part of a balanced approach—providing reliable energy today while supporting a cleaner energy future.
