Colorado's Future

Natural gas plays a valuable role in Colorado’s sustainable, lower-carbon energy future. It provides reliable energy to homes, businesses, and communities statewide. A balanced energy mix—including natural gas—helps promote dependable, efficient choices today and for generations to come.

Natural gas fuels over 2.1 million households and businesses across Colorado.*

Coloradans Value Energy Choice

Energy choice isn’t just a policy—it’s a principle. It means families can heat their homes reliably through Colorado’s long winters. It empowers local businesses to grow and thrive. And it ensures communities have the freedom to choose energy solutions that meet their unique needs. Energy choice fosters innovation, strengthens resilience, and gives Coloradans the flexibility to plan for the future on their own terms.

Reliability

In a state known for cold winters and heavy snowfall, reliable energy is a matter of safety and peace of mind. Natural gas provides dependable energy even during extreme weather, thanks to a robust underground pipeline network.

With natural gas, you get energy that works for you—no waiting, no interruptions—so you can keep your home comfortable and running smoothly all year long.

Read More

Versatility

Natural gas is a versatile energy source that fuels many of the appliances you use every day—such as water heaters, stoves, clothes dryers, and furnaces—with reliable performance. Whether you're cooking dinner, starting a load of laundry, or taking a hot shower, natural gas delivers consistent energy when you need it most.

Enjoying the benefits of high-efficiency natural gas appliances is made even more affordable with our rebates. From boilers to smart thermostats, you can save hundreds while improving your home’s efficiency and comfort.

Read More

Dependability

From small shops to large industrial operations, natural gas provides on-demand energy that supports Colorado businesses. In a state where business conditions can shift quickly, reliable energy gives owners the stability they need to operate smoothly day after day. Its versatility across commercial equipment also helps local businesses grow, adapt, and scale to meet their community’s needs.
